# Art placeholders

The prototype works without image files. It draws temporary item icons and character art when a PNG is missing.

When your illustrations are ready, place transparent PNG files at these paths:

## Items

- `assets/items/canned_food.png`
- `assets/items/pill.png`
- `assets/items/flashlight.png`
- `assets/items/computer.png`
- `assets/items/battery.png`
- `assets/items/duct_tape.png`
- `assets/items/energy_drink.png`
- `assets/items/data_core.png`
- `assets/items/star_fragment.png`
- `assets/items/medkit.png`

## Characters

- `assets/characters/silver.png`

Use square PNGs with transparent backgrounds where possible. The in-world sprites are billboarded toward the camera. Silver's placeholder occupies all three hand slots and is the only companion the capsule can carry.

The title screen, capsule, ship interior and interfaces are currently procedural 3D/CSS artwork; they do not need PNGs.
